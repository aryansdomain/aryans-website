Polybends

How to Play

An \(n\)-polybend is a shape whose boundary is made up of \(n\) congruent arcs. Each arc is \(1/6\) of a circle of radius 1, so it has length \(\pi/3\).

Suppose we take \(X\) polybends and join them, without overlap and along complete arcs only, into a bigger figure whose boundary has length \(Y\). Then this figure is said to have roundness \(X/Y\).

Play with polybends! Drag them from the sidebar and rotate them with the dot attached to each.

Adjust their shape in the settings menu.

Polybends was inspired by the Mathcamp 2026 Qualifying Quiz, Problem #2.
